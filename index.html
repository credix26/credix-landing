The problem

Sri Lanka's SMEs are trapped
in a permanent cash flow crisis.

You've done the work. You issued the invoice. But your business is stuck waiting on average 30, 60, even 90 days to see that money.

⏳

30 - 90 Day Payment Cycles

Buyers demand extended credit terms. You deliver the goods, issue the invoice, then wait months to be paid creating a permanent, compounding liquidity gap.

🏦

Banks are Too Slow and Exclusive

Traditional invoice discounting requires 1 - 3 weeks, heavy documentation, property collateral, and a CRIB score. Most SMEs don't qualify and those who do still wait too long.

💸

Overdrafts are a Debt Trap

~60% of Sri Lankan SMEs rely on high-interest overdrafts to survive cash flow gaps. You end up paying 18% - 24% per year to access money you've already earned.

📊

No Credit History = No Growth

Without a formal financial track record, SMEs remain invisible to capital. Every year without data is another year of being locked out of the credit system.
